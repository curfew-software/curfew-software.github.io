Privacy Policy — Curfew for Windows

_Last updated: 28 July 2026_

Short version: Curfew does not collect, transmit, or sell your browsing history or personal data. Blocking happens entirely on your computer.

What Curfew does not do

  • It does not record which sites or apps you use, and does not send your browsing history,

URLs, or app usage anywhere.

  • It does not contain third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

Data that stays on your device

  • Your configuration (the lockouts, schedules, block/allow lists, and settings) is stored locally

in C:\ProgramData\Curfew.

  • A local certificate (and its private key), used to show the HTTPS block page, is generated

and stored only on your computer; the private key never leaves your machine. Curfew does not use it to inspect, decrypt, or record your real web traffic — it only serves a "site blocked" page for sites you chose to block. This is optional and off until you agree to it: Curfew asks before installing anything, declining is treated as a refusal, and you can remove the certificate at any time from the tray icon menu. Uninstalling removes it too.

The only network requests Curfew makes

  1. Time synchronization (NTP). Curfew queries public time servers to get trusted time (so the

clock can't be changed to bypass a schedule). No personal data is sent.

  1. License validation — none. There isn't one. Your license key is verified entirely on your

device, using a public key built into the application. Curfew never sends your key anywhere, never contacts a licensing server, and does not know whether or how often you use it.

Payments

Purchases are handled by Payhip, which processes your payment and billing details under its own privacy policy. We never see or store your card details. From Payhip we receive only what is needed to deliver and support your purchase — typically your name, email address, and order reference.

Your rights

Because Curfew stores your configuration locally, you control it directly — edit or delete it in the app, or remove C:\ProgramData\Curfew. For questions about data held by our payment provider, contact us and we'll help.

Children

Curfew is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be noted on this page with a new date.
