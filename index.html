The difference

Most blockers ask nicely. Curfew doesn't.

A blocker you can switch off in a weak moment isn't a blocker. Curfew is built as a commitment device — the moment you'll want to cheat is the moment it holds.

01 — LOCKED MODE

No undo while it's running

Set a lockout to Locked and, while it's active, it can't be edited or deleted — not from the app, not by editing files, not even with your password. Future-you doesn't get a vote.

02 — BELOW THE BROWSER

Incognito & DoH can't slip past

Blocking happens at the system level, not as a browser extension. It covers every browser and private window, and it shuts the DNS-over-HTTPS back door that defeats ordinary hosts-file blockers.

03 — TAMPER-PROOF TIME

Changing the clock won't work

Schedules are pinned to trusted network time and a monotonic clock. Rolling the system clock forward or switching time zones to "end" a curfew early does nothing.

04 — ALWAYS ON

Keeps going after you close it

A background service enforces your curfews across reboots and after you quit the window — so closing the app isn't an escape hatch either.

Questions

The honest answers.

Why does Curfew install a certificate?

So it can show a proper "site blocked" page on HTTPS sites instead of a browser error. The certificate is created and stored only on your PC, its key never leaves the machine, and Curfew never inspects your real traffic. You can decline it (blocked sites then simply fail to connect), and uninstalling removes it completely.

Can I really not bypass it?

While a Locked curfew is active you can't edit or delete it, change the clock to skip it, or slip past it in a private window. Curfew isn't malware, though — like any app, an administrator can still uninstall it. It's a strong commitment device, not a prison.

Which browsers does it cover?

All of them — Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave, Opera and the rest — plus every private/incognito window. Because it blocks below the browser, there's no extension to disable.

Does it collect my data?

No. Blocking happens entirely on your computer. Curfew doesn't track or send your browsing history anywhere. It makes exactly one kind of network request: an NTP time check, so the clock can't be changed to escape a schedule. Your license key is verified on your own PC — it is never sent anywhere.

What do I need to run it?

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and administrator rights to install — Curfew needs those to enforce blocking system-wide. It's a small download and light on resources.
